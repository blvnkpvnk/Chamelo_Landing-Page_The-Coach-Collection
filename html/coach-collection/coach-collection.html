The problem

At one end you face straight into the sun; at the other it sits behind you. Cloud, shade, floodlights and a walk indoors change it again.

A fixed lens is right only part of the time, and photochromic lenses decide when to react. Eclipse™ puts the decision back in your hands.

Yes. Ace is a tennis-tuned shield with a high-contrast amber lens designed to sharpen the ball against court surfaces and LED backdrops, plus anti-fog and sweat-shedding coatings and a locked-in fit for rapid changes of direction.
Is Ace photochromic?
No. Eclipse™ lenses are electrochromic — they change on demand in 0.1 seconds when you move the slider, rather than reacting to UV like photochromic lenses.
